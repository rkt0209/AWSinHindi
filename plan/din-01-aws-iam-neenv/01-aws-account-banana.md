# 1️⃣ AWS Free Tier Account Banana — Step By Step

> Yahan **ek-ek click** likha hai, saath me "screen pe kya dikhega" bhi.
> Bilkul isi order me karo, kahin atkoge nahi.

📖 Official guide: https://docs.aws.amazon.com/accounts/latest/reference/manage-acct-creating.html
🆓 Free Tier page: https://aws.amazon.com/free/

---

## 🪜 STEP 1 — AWS Ki Website Kholo

- Browser (Chrome best) me jao: **https://aws.amazon.com/free/**
- Right upe **"Create a Free Account"** ya **"Create an AWS Account"** ka orange button dikhega.
- Us pe click karo.

**🖥️ Screen pe kya dikhega:** Ek form khulega jisme email maangega — heading hogi *"Sign up for AWS"*.

---

## 🪜 STEP 2 — Email Aur Account Ka Naam Daalo

- **Root user email address:** apni email daalo (ye tumhari malik wali ID hogi — sambhaal ke rakhna).
- **AWS account name:** koi bhi naam (jaise `Rohit-Personal` ya `MeraAWS`).
- **"Verify email address"** button dabao.

**🖥️ Screen pe:** "We sent a verification code to your email" — email pe ek **6-digit code (OTP)** aayega.

- Email kholo, code copy karo, form me daalo, **Verify** dabao.

> ⚠️ **Case: OTP nahi aaya?**
> - Spam/Junk folder check karo.
> - 2 min ruko, phir "Resend code" dabao.
> - Email sahi likhi hai? Dobara check karo.

---

## 🪜 STEP 3 — Root Password Banao

- Ek **strong password** banao (bade-chhote letter + number + symbol).
- Confirm karo. **Continue** dabao.

**🖥️ Screen pe:** Ab "Contact Information" ka page khulega.

> 💡 Password kahin safe likh lo (phone note/diary). Bhoolna nahi.

---

## 🪜 STEP 4 — Apni Jaankari (Contact Info)

- **Account type** poochega: **Personal** chuno (student/intern ke liye yahi sahi hai).
  - *(Business tab chuno jab company ke liye ho.)*
- Naam, phone number, country (**India**), address, city, pincode daalo.
- Terms wale checkbox pe ✅ tick karo. **Continue** dabao.

**🖥️ Screen pe:** Ab "Billing Information" (card) ka page aayega.

---

## 🪜 STEP 5 — Card Daalo (Verification Ke Liye)

- **Debit/Credit card** number, expiry, CVV daalo.
- Billing address wahi rakho jo upar diya.
- **Verify and Continue** dabao.

**🖥️ Screen pe / phone pe:** Bank ka **OTP** aayega (₹2–₹15 ka verification). Ye **wapas aa jaata hai**.

> ⚠️ **All possible cases yahan:**
> - **"Card declined":** International transaction ON karo (bank app me), ya doosra card try karo. RuPay kabhi-kabhi kaam nahi karta — Visa/Mastercard best.
> - **Chhota charge dikha:** Ghabrana nahi, ye **refundable verification** hai, kat ke wapas aa jaata hai.
> - **3D-secure OTP:** phone pe aaye OTP daalo.

---

## 🪜 STEP 6 — Phone/Identity Verify Karo

- **Text message (SMS)** ya **Voice call** chuno.
- Phone number daalo, captcha bharo, **Send SMS** dabao.
- Phone pe aaye **code** ko daalo. **Continue** dabao.

**🖥️ Screen pe:** "Your identity has been verified successfully" ✅

---

## 🪜 STEP 7 — Support Plan Chuno (FREE Wala)

- 3 option aayenge: **Basic support - Free**, Developer, Business.
- **"Basic support - Free"** chuno (₹0). **Complete sign up** dabao.

**🖥️ Screen pe:** *"Congratulations! Thank you for signing up with AWS."* 🎉

---

## 🪜 STEP 8 — Console Me Login Karo

- **"Go to the AWS Management Console"** button dabao.
- Login page pe **"Root user"** chuno → email daalo → password daalo → **Sign in**.

**🖥️ Screen pe:** AWS **Console Home** khulega — bahut saari services ke naam, ek search bar upar. Ye tumhara "dashboard" hai. 🏠

> ⚠️ **Case: "Account is being activated" ya "under review":**
> - Kabhi-kabhi account activate hone me **kuch minute se 24 ghante** lag jaate hain.
> - Email aayega jab ready ho jaye. Tab tak intezaar karo — ye normal hai, ghabrana nahi.
