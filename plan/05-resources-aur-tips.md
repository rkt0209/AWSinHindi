## 🆓 Free Resources (Yahin Se Seekho)

### Sabse Pehle — Official AWS (free aur best)
- **AWS Skill Builder** (skillbuilder.aws) — AWS ka apna free learning platform. Yahan har service ke free course hain.
- **AWS Free Tier** (aws.amazon.com/free) — practice ke liye free account. **Card lagega**, par free tier limit me paise nahi katenge.
- **AWS Documentation** — har service ka "Getting Started" guide. Boring par sabse sahi.

### YouTube (Hindi/aasaan)
- AWS ke topics Hindi me search karo: *"AWS S3 kya hai Hindi"*, *"AWS Lambda tutorial Hindi"*.
- Channels jaise **"AWS in Hindi"**, **"Technical Guftgu"** type ke — easy explanation milti hai.
- English thoda comfortable ho to: **freeCodeCamp**, **TechWorld with Nana** (best for beginners).

### Hands-on Practice
- **AWS Workshops** (workshops.aws) — step-by-step practical labs, bilkul free.

## 💰 Paise Bachane Ke Rules (BAHUT ZAROORI)

1. **Hamesha Free Tier** services use karo.
2. **EMR cluster** banao to kaam ke baad **TURANT terminate** karo (ye mehnga hai!).
3. Practice ke baad jo banaya use **delete/stop** kar do (Lambda, S3 files, etc.).
4. AWS me **Billing Alert** laga do — ₹1 bhi kate to email aa jaye.
5. Root account ki jagah **IAM user** se kaam karo (safe).

## 🎓 Day-1 (Joining) Ke Liye Killer Tips

1. **Sab kuch yaad karne ki zaroorat nahi.** Tum intern ho — basic samajh kaafi hai. Confidence rakho.

2. **Real-life examples zubaani taiyaar rakho.** Koi pooche "S3 kya hai?" → "Internet ka godown jahan files rakhte hain." Aisa jawab impress karta hai.

3. **Achhe sawaal taiyaar karo** (ye sabse bada plus point hai):
   - "Hamare team me data pipeline me zyada Glue use hota hai ya EMR?"
   - "Step Functions me error handling ka aap log kaise pattern follow karte ho?"
   - "Spec/steering files ke liye koi standard template hai team ka?"
   > Achhe sawaal = "ye intern serious hai" ka signal. 👍

4. **"Mujhe nahi aata" se mat daro.** Bolo "abhi seekh raha hoon, basics clear hain." Honesty + seekhne ka attitude = best impression.

5. **Notes banao roz.** Jo Day 1-10 me seekha, ek chhoti file me likh lo — interview/review me kaam aayega.

6. **Pehle din zyada bolne se zyada sun-no aur observe karo.** Team kaise kaam karti hai, wo dekho.
